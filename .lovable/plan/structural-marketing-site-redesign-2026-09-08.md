# Structural marketing-site redesign

## Scope
Rebuild the public marketing presentation only. Preserve all routes, page copy, booking behaviour, admin dashboard, authentication, data, and backend schema.

## Build
- Replace the global visual system with exactly five colours: blue `#335CAA`, white `#FFFFFF`, ink `#14171C`, grey `#5B6472`, and hairline `#E4E6EA`; remove gold, brass, cream, beige, gradients, and obsolete colour aliases from public presentation.
- Load Cormorant Garamond at weight 500 for headings and Inter at weights 400–600 for all body and interface text.
- Rebuild the shared header, page introductions, footer, CTA band, floating actions, and buttons to match the restrained editorial reference. Keep the smooth mobile-menu behaviour.
- Rebuild the homepage hero as an asymmetric text-and-portrait composition, followed by a three-statement trust row, a border-separated editorial service list, an asymmetric doctor section, a distinct numbered patient journey, supporting proof content, and one full-width dark pull-quote.
- Rebuild the services index as a pure editorial row list with service name, description, and link—no cards, shadows, icons, or badges.
- Recompose About, service-detail, Contact, and Privacy pages into distinct left-aligned editorial layouts using hairline divisions and whitespace rather than repeated cards.
- Remove every eyebrow/rule heading pattern, isolated headline colour accent, middle-dot separator, repeated rounded-shadow card, and circular badge from public marketing code.

## Validation
- Scan the public presentation code for forbidden colours, separators, eyebrow props/utilities, gradients, shadows, and badge usage.
- Check all public pages at desktop and mobile sizes, including the mobile menu, for layout, overflow, legibility, and console errors.
- Confirm booking and admin files and behaviour remain untouched.
